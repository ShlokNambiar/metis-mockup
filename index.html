METIS · INTERFACE PROTOTYPE

Agents that can be trusted
with regulated work.

Metis runs AI agents on back-office operations for Indian insurers and asset managers — policy renewals, data extraction, reconciliation — and produces a regulator-ready record of every action they take.

Fourteen screens. Sample data throughout; Sahyadri Insurance Services and Deccan General are fictional. Two screens are interactive — they are marked below.

THE CONSOLE · ADMIN TO ANALYST

Not five apps. One app that hides what you are not accountable for.

OPERATE Today — the floor Your agents, what each is on, what is waiting on you. Click a name and everything narrows to their day. interactive · follow an agent STRUCTURE One app, five doors The same console from five chairs. Separation of duties is the shape of the app, not a setting inside it. OPERATE Analyst — the whole app Three nav items and one queue. Most of the company works here, and most of the company should never meet a settings page. BUILD Workflow studio A workflow as an editable document, not code. Autonomy tier per step, and the halt gate locked by compliance. BUILD Model routing & policy The data class decides the model, not the developer. Unclassified data is refused rather than quietly routed. BUILD Connections Every system an agent can reach and exactly how far in — including one insurer with no API at all. MONITOR Runs, halts and cost Where cases keep getting stuck is the roadmap. 61 of 148 halts trace to one missing system. GOVERN People, roles & config audit Changing a setting is itself a ledger event, in the same chain as the work it governs.

Two rules run through every screen. Blue means traceable — it is never used for an ordinary button, only for something you can follow back to its source. And a halt is not an error: when a value is missing the agent stops instead of guessing, so it is coloured as a request for help, never as a failure.
